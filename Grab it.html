Why Grab it

Not a general delivery app. A campus specialist.

  • Product selection based on what students actually need.
  • Shorter delivery zones and familiar drop-off points.
  • Demand that follows class schedules, exams and hostel routines.
  • Partnering with existing campus stores instead of replacing them.
Launch plan

Start small. Learn fast. Expand carefully.

1. Pilot

One campus zone and a limited set of high-demand products.

2. Measure

Track order volume, delivery time, repeat customers and popular items.

3. Expand

Improve the service, add products and extend coverage if the pilot works.

Challenges we’re planning for: campus permissions, inventory, delivery staffing and reliable fulfilment.
