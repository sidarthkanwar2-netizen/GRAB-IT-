# 🛒 Grabitr

**Grabitr** is a campus-focused quick-commerce concept designed to make everyday essentials accessible to college students without the hassle of leaving campus.

Think of it as a mini quick-commerce platform built specifically for campus life.

## 🚀 About the Project

Grabitr aims to provide students with a convenient way to order snacks, stationery, beverages, and other daily essentials through a simple online platform.

The idea is to combine the speed of quick commerce with the convenience of a campus-based delivery system.

## ✨ Features

- 🛍️ Browse everyday essentials
- 🔎 Simple and user-friendly interface
- 🏫 Campus-focused shopping experience
- ⚡ Quick and convenient ordering concept
- 📱 Designed with a modern web interface

## 🎯 Our Vision

To make campus life easier by bringing essential products closer to students and creating a faster, more convenient shopping experience within college campuses.

## 💡 Business Model

Grabitr follows a campus-based quick-commerce model using a small inventory hub (dark store) to fulfil student orders efficiently.
